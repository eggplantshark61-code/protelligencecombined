Schedule 03 — Method

A valuation that cannot be reproduced is an opinion. A valuation that can is evidence. The process below runs the same way for every engagement, every client, every side.

Working Papers Retained

Conflict Check

Parties, counsel, and carriers are screened before any records are opened. If independence is compromised, the engagement is declined.

Records & Data Intake

Repair orders, photographs, carrier valuation reports, title history, and market data — logged, indexed, and preserved.

Valuation Analysis

Comparable selection, condition adjustment, and market reconciliation performed under a documented, repeatable framework.

Defensible Report

Findings written for the record: sources cited, adjustments itemized, conclusions stated in plain terms a trier of fact can follow.

Testimony Support

Deposition preparation, exhibits, and trial testimony when the matter requires the analysis to stand up and speak.

Schedule 07 — Questions

Asked and
answered.

Do you work for plaintiffs or for defendants?

Both, though never on the same matter and never with a conflict. The methodology is identical regardless of who retains the practice; that symmetry is what makes the work useful in litigation.

What does a CCC or Mitchell review actually examine?

Comparable vehicle selection, condition ratings, itemized adjustments, and the arithmetic that connects them. The review states where the report holds and where it does not — whichever way that cuts.

Will you testify, or only write reports?

Both. Reports are written from the first line as if they will be examined under oath, and deposition or trial testimony is available whenever the matter requires it.

What is needed to open a file?

Party names for the conflict check, then the records: valuation reports, repair documentation, photographs, and title history. Intake is itemized so counsel always knows what has been received.

Where do you take engagements?

Nationwide. Records-based valuation work travels well, and testimony logistics are arranged per matter.
