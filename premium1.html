FIG 03 — PROCESS SCHEMATIC

A fixed sequence, from conflict check to the witness stand.

STEP 1

Conflict Check

Parties, carriers, and counsel are screened before any substantive discussion. Independence is confirmed first or the engagement does not proceed.

STEP 2

Records & Data Intake

Valuation reports, repair records, photographs, and title history are catalogued into a single documented case file.

STEP 3

Valuation Analysis

Market data is gathered, comparables vetted, and adjustments applied under a transparent, reproducible methodology.

STEP 4

Defensible Report

Findings are issued in a written report that states its data, its reasoning, and its limits — nothing left to inference.

STEP 5

Testimony Support

Where matters proceed, the analysis is defended at deposition or trial, consistent with the written record.

FIG 08 — FREQUENT INQUIRIES

Questions counsel and claimants ask first.

Do you work for policyholders or for insurers?+
Both — though never in the same matter, and never with a standing allegiance to either. Independence is the product. Each engagement begins with a conflict check, and the methodology is identical regardless of who retains the practice.
What records should we have ready before a case review?+
Whatever exists: the carrier's valuation report (CCC, Mitchell, or similar), repair estimates and invoices, photographs, the police or incident report, and the vehicle's title and service history. An incomplete file is not a barrier — intake identifies gaps and how to close them.
Are you available for deposition and trial testimony?+
Yes. Expert reports are written with testimony in mind, and the analysis presented in deposition or at trial is the same analysis stated in the written report — no revisions between the page and the stand.
Do you take matters outside your home state?+
The practice is national. Vehicle markets are documented from verifiable data sources regardless of venue, and remote records intake means geography rarely affects scope or schedule.
How is the engagement priced?+
Fees are quoted in writing after the initial case review, once scope is defined. Pricing is never contingent on the valuation conclusion — a structural safeguard of the opinion's independence.
