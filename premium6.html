§ 2.0 — Engagements Undertaken

Five clauses define
the whole practice.

Nothing outside this scope is accepted; nothing inside it is delegated. Each clause carries the same specification: documented inputs, reproducible analysis, a conclusion that survives cross-examination.

§ 2.1

Total-Loss Valuation Disputes

Re-derivation of the settlement figure from verifiable market evidence. The carrier's number is checked against the record — and confirmed or corrected accordingly.

Deliverable: Valuation report, comparables schedule, adjustment detail
§ 2.2

Diminished-Value Claims

Quantification of the market penalty a repair history imposes at resale — measured, documented, and stated as a defensible figure rather than a rounded guess.

Deliverable: Diminished-value analysis with supporting market data
§ 2.3

Expert Reports & Testimony

Written opinions prepared for disclosure, deposition, and trial. The analyst who drafted the report presents and defends it in person.

Deliverable: Expert report, exhibits, deposition & trial testimony
§ 2.4

Estate, Bankruptcy & Divorce Valuation

Neutral valuations for fiduciary and family-court contexts, where the number must serve the record rather than either party.

Deliverable: Court-ready valuation, dated and sourced
§ 2.5

Insurance Claim & CCC/Mitchell Review

Clause-by-clause examination of carrier valuation reports: comparable selection, condition ratings, and every adjustment between the cover page and the total.

Deliverable: Written review with itemized findings

§ 7.0 — Requests for Information

Answered
and logged.

RFI-01Do you work for plaintiffs or defendants?

Both — never on the same matter, and never through a conflict. The methodology is identical regardless of caption, which is precisely what makes the work admissible and useful to either side.

RFI-02What does a CCC/Mitchell review cover?

Comparable selection, condition ratings, itemized adjustments, and the arithmetic connecting them. Findings state where the report holds and where it fails — in whichever direction the evidence points.

RFI-03Do you provide testimony?

Yes. Reports are drafted from the first clause as testimony-ready documents, and deposition or trial appearance is available whenever the matter requires it.

RFI-04What is required to open a file?

Party names for the conflict check, then the record set: valuation reports, repair documentation, photographs, and title history. Intake is inventoried, so counsel always knows what the file contains.

RFI-05What is your service area?

National. Records-based valuation carries no geographic penalty, and testimony logistics are arranged per matter.
