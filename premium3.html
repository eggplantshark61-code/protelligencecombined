SECTION 01 — EXHIBITS: SCOPE OF ENGAGEMENT

Five engagement types. Each one enters the record ready to be examined.

Every matter is logged, scoped in writing, and prepared to a documentary standard — because in litigation, a valuation that cannot show its work is not a valuation at all.

EXHIBIT ATL-DISPUTE

Total-Loss Valuation Disputes

Independent determination of actual cash value when the settlement offer and the market disagree. Comparables identified, adjustments itemized, sources preserved for examination.

METHOD: MARKET-EVIDENCEFORMAT: WRITTEN REPORT
EXHIBIT BDIM-VALUE

Diminished-Value Claims

Measurement of the post-repair market penalty: pre-loss value against post-repair value, established from observable market behavior rather than convenience formulas.

BASIS: PRE/POST DELTAFORMAT: WRITTEN REPORT
EXHIBIT CEXPERT

Expert Reports & Testimony

Opinions drafted for disclosure and defended at deposition or trial — the testimony never departs from the written record.

VENUE: DEPO / TRIAL
EXHIBIT DFIDUCIARY

Estate, Bankruptcy & Divorce Valuation

Neutral, dated valuations for courts, trustees, and dividing parties — a figure with no side to favor.

USE: FILINGS / DIVISION
EXHIBIT EAUDIT

Insurance Claim & CCC / Mitchell Review

Line-item audit of carrier valuation reports: comparable selection, condition ratings, and adjustments tested against the market record.

OUTPUT: FINDINGS MEMO

SECTION 06 — INTERROGATORIES, ANSWERED

The questions every retaining party asks. Answered for the record.

ROG 01State whether the practice works for policyholders or insurers.›
ResponseBoth — never in the same matter, and never under a standing allegiance. Each engagement opens with a conflict check, and the methodology is identical whichever side retains the practice. Independence is the deliverable.
ROG 02Identify the records required before a case review.›
ResponseThe carrier's valuation report (CCC, Mitchell, or similar), repair estimates and invoices, photographs, any police or incident report, and title and service history. An incomplete file is workable — intake exists to find and cure the gaps.
ROG 03State whether deposition and trial testimony are available.›
ResponseYes. Reports are drafted in anticipation of examination, and testimony tracks the written opinion exactly. There is no version of the analysis reserved for the stand.
ROG 04State the geographic scope of the practice.›
ResponseNationwide. Market evidence is documented from verifiable sources in any venue, and records intake is handled remotely, so jurisdiction rarely affects scope or schedule.
ROG 05Describe how fees are determined.›
ResponseFees are quoted in writing once scope is defined at the initial case review — and are never contingent on the valuation conclusion. An opinion priced by its answer would not survive its first deposition, and should not.
