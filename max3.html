SEC.02 ENGAGEMENT INDEX

Five instruments. One standard of proof.

Each engagement type follows the same discipline: independent position, stated methodology, documented sources, and a conclusion that another professional could reproduce. The subject changes; the standard doesn't.

Discuss a Matter

  1. 01
    ENG.01 · ACTUAL CASH VALUE

    Total-Loss Valuation Disputes

    When a vehicle is declared a total loss, the settlement rests on a single number — and that number is frequently built from thin comparables and unexamined condition assumptions. We rebuild actual cash value from the ground up: verified comparable sales, documented options and mileage, condition evidence from the record. Where the carrier's valuation holds, we say so. Where it doesn't, we show precisely why.

    • ACV RECONSTRUCTION
    • COMPARABLE AUDIT
    • CONDITION EVIDENCE
  2. 02
    ENG.02 · POST-REPAIR LOSS

    Diminished-Value Claims

    A properly repaired vehicle with an accident history is worth less than its unblemished twin — the question is how much, and whether the figure survives cross-examination. We measure the gap with market data rather than formula shortcuts, and we document the path from comparable to conclusion so the number stands on evidence, not assertion.

    • INHERENT DV
    • REPAIR-RELATED DV
    • MARKET EVIDENCE
  3. 03
    ENG.03 · THE WITNESS STAND

    Expert Reports & Testimony

    An opinion that cannot be explained under oath is not worth commissioning. Our reports are structured for litigation from the first page: qualifications, methodology, data, analysis, conclusion — each element separable, each source citable. We support counsel through disclosure, deposition, and trial, and we answer the hard questions the same way we answer the easy ones.

    • WRITTEN REPORTS
    • DEPOSITION
    • TRIAL TESTIMONY
  4. 04
    ENG.04 · DATE-OF-VALUE MATTERS

    Estate, Bankruptcy & Divorce Valuation

    Courts, trustees, and opposing counsel all ask the same thing of a vehicle valuation: what was it worth, on that date, supported by what? We produce neutral fair-market valuations tied to a specific date and a documented market — for probate inventories, bankruptcy schedules, and marital estates where a single vehicle, or a collection, needs a defensible number.

    • FMV AS-OF DATE
    • PROBATE & TRUSTS
    • MARITAL ESTATES
  5. 05
    ENG.05 · SOFTWARE VS. MARKET

    Insurance Claim & CCC/Mitchell Review

    Valuation platforms are tools, not oracles. Behind every CCC or Mitchell figure sit choices — which comparables, which condition grades, which adjustments — and those choices can be examined. We take the estimate apart line by line and report where the output is sound, where it drifts from the observable market, and what the difference is worth.

    • LINE-ITEM AUDIT
    • ADJUSTMENT REVIEW
    • PLATFORM ANALYSIS

SEC.08 INSPECTION LOG · COMMON QUESTIONS 5 ENTRIES

Questions we answer before you ask.

Q.01What do your services cost?

Fees are discussed after the conflict check, once we understand the scope of the matter — a single-vehicle diminished-value opinion and a multi-vehicle estate are different undertakings. What we can promise up front: the fee structure is stated in writing before work begins, and it never depends on the outcome of your case. Contingent fees and valuation work don't mix.

Q.02Do you take cases in my state?

Yes. PROTELLIGENCE is a national practice — engagements are accepted in all fifty states. Much of the work (records review, market analysis, report preparation) is performed remotely by design; inspections and testimony are handled on-site where the matter requires it.

Q.03What records do you need to begin?

The short list: the valuation or estimate in dispute (CCC, Mitchell, or otherwise), repair records and invoices, photographs, and the vehicle's title and options information. The fuller the record, the tighter the tolerance — but we routinely work matters where the file is thin and say plainly what the gaps do to certainty.

Q.04Will you testify, or only write reports?

Both, and by the same person. The professional who performs the analysis writes the report and gives the testimony — conference, deposition, or trial. An expert who won't defend the work under oath hasn't finished the work.

Q.05You work for insurers and plaintiffs. How is that independent?

That's precisely what makes it independent. The methodology is fixed before the client is known: same data standards, same disclosure, same tolerances, whichever side of the caption engages us. A conflict check precedes every matter, and if the evidence doesn't support the number a client hoped for, the report says so. That is the product.
